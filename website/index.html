FAQ

Why 2.26 GB, not 2.9?

The release was an older, heavier build. CI builds are newer and leaner.

Flash the USB stick?

No. Ventoy once, then ISOs are plain files you copy.

Does it touch my disk?

No. Live from RAM + USB. Installing is an explicit choice.

Minimum specs?

64-bit CPU, 4 GB RAM, UEFI or BIOS. Every build is QEMU-boot verified.
